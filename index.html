Paint Correction &
Ceramic Coating.

When a wash and wax isn't enough. We polish out what's in your paint, then lock in the shine with a coating that lasts for years. All done at your location.

Which One Fits Your Vehicle?

Paint Enhancement

A single-step polish that boosts gloss and shine. It won't remove most defects, but it's a noticeable refresh for a car that's been well kept.

Paint Correction

A multi-stage polish that permanently removes swirls, scratches, and other defects, bringing back depth and clarity. The highest level of finish.

Ceramic Coating

A liquid layer that cures into a hard, water-repellent barrier against scratches, swirls, UV rays, and road contaminants. Washing gets easier and the gloss stays.
